About Us

Café Luna is a small, cozy coffee bar where quality coffee meets a relaxed atmosphere. We believe in simplicity, good taste and friendly service.

Our coffee is carefully selected from trusted local and international roasters, and every cup is prepared with attention and passion. Whether you are stopping by for a quick espresso or spending time with friends, Café Luna is a place to slow down and enjoy the moment.
